# FV07 — Independent visible redness research handoff

Research only; no FV07 implementation in FV06. Optical eligibility is necessary but not sufficient for skin-color measurement. Maintain versioned input contract and separate research output; no product verdict or clinical claim.

1. Establish the measurement target: repeatable visible skin color (e.g., calibrated CIE L*a*b* a* or a reference-relative color contrast), not inflammation, disease, efficacy or diagnosis. Define units, reference, illumination and capture context before choosing a score.
2. Capture and preprocessing: investigate browser camera color management, transfer function, tone mapping, exposure/white-balance drift, compression and device-specific color responses. Use a neutral/color target and controlled illumination to test calibration; do not assume RGB values or brightness matching guarantee comparable color. Exclude specular, saturated, shadowed and occluded pixels. Quantify image processing effects.
3. ROI: use landmarks to align consistent cheek/forehead regions and exclude eyes, lips, hair, makeup and background; validate ROI stability under scale/pose changes and separately evaluate segmentation. Current optical proxies are eligibility signals only. Explicitly test pose/scale coupling observed in FV06.
4. Reference: recruit consented participants spanning skin-tone ranges (measured reference color, not inferred race), camera models, lighting and days. Use calibrated colorimeter/spectrophotometer or validated color imaging under documented illumination; color targets establish instrument response, not skin biology. Public datasets only if licensing, consent, acquisition and reference labels are suitable; do not assume a clinical dataset has calibrated color.
5. Validation: preregister sample-size rationale, held-out subjects/devices, repeated same-session and between-day captures, known perturbations, reference agreement, bias, within-person SD, repeatability coefficient and limits of agreement. Report uncertainty, missingness, abstention and subgroup error across skin tones and devices. Avoid training/validation leakage; no tuning on final holdout.
6. Abstain when optical conditions fail, reference absent or unreliable, exposure/white balance uncertain, skin regions unstable, clipping/shadows/makeup/occlusion severe, model/device unsupported or uncertainty exceeds predeclared limits. Establish color-specific eligibility beyond FV06. No numeric output until measurement and uncertainty thresholds supported by evidence.
7. Engineering next step: offline, consented calibration harness with synthetic color patches and reference-target captures; record provenance and numbers without remote facial-image storage. Keep production YouCam and FV06 contract isolated. Future changes to measurement or thresholds require version increment and evidence.

FV06 hardware evidence supports the acquisition foundation only. FV07 can be planned and offline research prepared while final engineering gates remain pending; do not call FV06 fully accepted until exact-head automated and physical restart checks pass.
